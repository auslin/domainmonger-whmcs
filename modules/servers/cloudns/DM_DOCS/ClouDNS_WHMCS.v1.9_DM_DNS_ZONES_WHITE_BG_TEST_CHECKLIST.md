# DNS Zones White Background Test Checklist

1. Open the product/client area DNS Zones list page from Overview.
2. Confirm the DNS Zones content area has a white background with rounded corners.
3. Confirm the `DNS Zones` heading appears inside the white panel.
4. Confirm the `+Add` button remains visible when registered domains are not enabled.
5. Confirm Manage opens the selected zone's DNS records page.
6. Confirm Delete still prompts for confirmation and targets the selected zone.
7. Confirm the page remains usable on a narrow/mobile viewport.
